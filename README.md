## 🧠 Overview

**ShaperBox 3** from Cableguys is an advanced tool designed to give music producers and sound designers full control over dynamic audio shaping. It provides a powerful suite of effects that allow users to manipulate volume, filters, panning, and more in an intuitive and customizable way.

With ShaperBox 3, you can apply rhythmic effects, modulate various parameters in real-time, and add movement to otherwise static sounds. Its low-frequency oscillators (LFOs) and envelope-following technology make it easy to create complex, evolving textures and rhythms.

This plugin is perfect for musicians, sound designers, and DJs who want to inject creativity and complexity into their mixes. It works seamlessly with most DAWs, offering the flexibility to perform live or refine tracks in the studio.
